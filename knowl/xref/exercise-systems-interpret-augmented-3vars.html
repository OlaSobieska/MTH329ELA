Exercise 15.

\(\displaystyle \begin{abmatrix}{rrr|r} 1 \amp -3 \amp -2 \amp 5 \\ 0 \amp 1 \amp 3 \amp -3 \\ 0 \amp 0 \amp 1 \amp 2 \end{abmatrix}\)
🔗
Solution.
Using variables \(x,y,z\) the corresponding system is
\begin{equation*} \begin{sysofeqns}{rcrcrcr} x \amp - \amp 3 y \amp - \amp 2 z \amp = \amp 5 \\ \amp \amp y \amp + \amp 3 z \amp = \amp -3 \\ \amp \amp \amp \amp z \amp = \amp 2 \end{sysofeqns}\text{.} \end{equation*}
From the third equation, we immediately have \(z = 2\text{,}\) and we can substitute that value into the second equation to obtain
\begin{gather*} y + 3 z = -3 \\ y + 3 \cdot 2 = -3 \\ y + 6 = -3 \\ y = -9 \text{.} \end{gather*}
Knowing values for both \(y\) and \(z\text{,}\) we can substitute them into the first equation to obtain
\begin{gather*} x - 3 y - 2 z = 5 \\ x - 3 \cdot (-9) - 2 \cdot 2 = 5 \\ x + 23 = 5 \\ x = - 18 \text{.} \end{gather*}
So the solution to the system is \(x = -18, y = -9, z = 2\text{.}\)
